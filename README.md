Cuisine Combination Analysis

📌 Objective

Identify the most common combinations of cuisines in the restaurant dataset and determine whether certain cuisine combinations tend to receive higher aggregate ratings.

🎯 Analysis Goals

Identify the most frequently occurring cuisine combinations.

Calculate the average aggregate rating for each cuisine combination.

Compare ratings across different cuisine combinations.

Identify combinations associated with higher customer ratings.

Understand customer preferences for restaurants offering multiple cuisines.

🛠️ Tools Used

Tableau

Microsoft Excel

Data Visualization

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

📊 Key Analysis

1. Most Common Cuisine Combinations

Group restaurants by their cuisine combinations.

Count the number of restaurants for each combination.

Identify the top 10 most common combinations.

Present the results using a bar chart in Tableau.

2. Average Rating by Cuisine Combination

Calculate the average aggregate rating for each cuisine combination.

Compare average ratings across combinations.

Use a bar chart to identify combinations with higher average ratings.

Filter combinations with very few restaurants to avoid misleading comparisons.

📈 Suggested Tableau Visualizations

Top 10 Cuisine Combinations: Bar chart showing the most common combinations and restaurant counts.

Average Rating by Cuisine: Bar chart comparing average aggregate ratings.

Cuisine Combination Dashboard: Combine restaurant counts and average ratings into an interactive dashboard.

💡 Expected Insights

The most popular cuisine combinations in the dataset.

Cuisine combinations with higher average restaurant ratings.

Differences between popular combinations and highly rated combinations.

Potential relationships between cuisine variety and customer satisfaction.

🏁 Conclusion

This analysis explores how cuisine combinations vary across restaurants and whether particular combinations are associated with higher aggregate ratings. Tableau visualizations help communicate restaurant popularity and rating patterns.
